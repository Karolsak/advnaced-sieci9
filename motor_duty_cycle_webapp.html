Mathematical Models:

RMS Power Calculation:
PRMS = √(Σ(Pi² × ti) / Σ(ti))

For Linearly Varying Load:
PRMS = √((P₁² + P₁×P₂ + P₂²) / 3)

Differential Equations:
Electrical: L·dI/dt = V - Eb - I·R(T)
Mechanical: J·dω/dt = Tem - Tfriction
Thermal: Cth·dT/dt = Ploss - (T-Tamb)/Rth
